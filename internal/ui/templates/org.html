Policy

Organization policy may only tighten the platform baseline. The server refuses a document that loosens maxAutoPublishSeverity, wildcardEgress or requireHumanReview; every other key it keeps at the stricter of the two values, and a whenCapabilities condition is added to the baseline's. This page does not show the baseline; read it at GET /api/v1/platform/policy.

The server reads these keys: requireHumanReview, blockedCapabilities, blockedMcpServers, allowedLicenses, noPublicLicense, maxAutoPublishSeverity, requireEvals, wildcardEgress. It stores any other key and shows it here, but never enforces it.

Each form shows what is stored: the SAML metadata, the OIDC issuer and client id, and whether the connection is enabled. The client secret is never shown; leave it blank to keep the stored one, and remove the connection to drop it. Test reports the probe's verdict here.

Your IdP fetches this organization's SP metadata from /api/v1/orgs/<handle>/sso/saml/metadata. Test checks the stored configuration and the IdP's discovery document; it never sends the client secret.

{{if not .Org.Decommissioning}}

Decommission

Decommissioning ends the organization. Its members keep reading its agents and exporting its audit log during the hold, and nothing else can change; when the hold ends its agents, repositories, archives and invitations are erased, every membership ends, and its handle and domain are released. Every published release is yanked first. This is the one way an organization loses its last admin.
